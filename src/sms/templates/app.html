Installing it

  1. Open this page on the tablet and press the button.
  2. Android will ask whether to allow installing apps from your browser. Say yes — this file comes from your own server on your own network.
  3. Open the app and enter this server's address and a token.

Updating is the same thing again: press the button and it installs over the old one, keeping the address and token you already entered.

A token

Make one on the server, and paste it into the app's settings:

sms token create "tablet" --scope catalog:read --scope catalog:write

catalog:read alone is enough to browse and read; catalog:write is what lets the app save annotations.
